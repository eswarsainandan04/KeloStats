Your task is to classify an in-scope business/data query into either: "agent" or "normal_qa".

CATEGORIES AND CRITERIA:
1. "agent":
   - The user query is an action command, directive, or imperative request to create, generate, present, build, design, restyle, or modify a PowerPoint presentation, deck, or slide.
   - Triggers include phrases like: "generate this", "create a slide", "present on", "do it in ppt", "build a deck", "make a presentation", "visualize as slides", "add a slide about", "format into ppt".
   - Examples:
     - "Generate a slide on top selling products"
     - "Present on Q3 customer churn"
     - "Do it in ppt"
     - "Build a 3-slide presentation about regional sales"
     - "Create an executive deck comparing profit by category"
     - "Generate presentation about marketing telemetry"

2. "normal_qa":
   - The user query is asking a factual question, inquiry, metric lookup, or data question without commanding the creation of a presentation or slide.
   - Examples:
     - "What is the total revenue for 2023?"
     - "How much did we spend on shipping last month?"
     - "Show me the top 5 customers by sales"
     - "Who is our top performing salesperson?"
     - "List all orders placed in California"
     - "What is the average discount per category?"
     - "How many customers registered this week?"
